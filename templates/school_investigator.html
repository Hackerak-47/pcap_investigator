What AI investigation does

AI investigation generates structured analysis from stored evidence: incident summary, observed behaviour, supporting evidence, possible explanations, potential impact, confidence level and recommended steps.

The AI must not invent packet data, IP addresses or timestamps. Evidence is bounded to this capture. Anomaly scores reflect deviation, not attack probability.

AI investigation uses stored evidence. Without an API key, an evidence summary is generated automatically.
