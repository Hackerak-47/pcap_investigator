Architecture & Detection Methodology

System design, threat heuristics, risk scoring formulas, and ethical limitations for academic demonstration.

1. End-to-End Processing Architecture
Raw PCAP File → PyShark Dissector → SQLite Packet Storage → Detection Engine (6 rules) → Temporal Correlation → AI Synthesis & PDF Report

The application incrementally parses packets into structured SQLite schemas with secondary indexing on (analysis_id, src_ip, dst_ip, protocol, timestamp), preventing out-of-memory crashes on large captures.

2. Threat Detection Rules & Configurable Thresholds
Detector Trigger Heuristics Default Threshold Target Phase
PORT_SCAN Rapid TCP connection attempts to many unique destination ports (SYN ratio > 70%) ≥ 10 unique ports / 60s Reconnaissance
SSH_BRUTE_FORCE Repeated SYN attempts to TCP port 22 within a sliding temporal window ≥ 10 attempts / 120s Credential Access
DNS_ANOMALY Shannon entropy > 3.8, label lengths > 40 chars, or burst rate > 100/min Entropy > 3.8 / Label > 40 chars Exfiltration / C2
SUSPICIOUS_OUTBOUND Internal client connecting to known backdoor ports (4444, 31337, etc.) or high fan-out Fanout > 50 or port in Blacklist Command & Control
ICMP_ANOMALY High burst of ICMP Echo requests across one or multiple targets ≥ 50 packets / 10s Reconnaissance Sweep
HTTP_ANOMALY Abnormal HTTP verbs (CONNECT/TRACE/TRACK) or excessive request bursts ≥ 200 reqs / 60s Web Exploitation

Forensic & Legal Disclaimers
  • PCAP Limitations: Packet captures demonstrate network traffic transmission, but cannot prove application-layer authentication outcomes or process execution on endpoints without server syslog/EDR correlation.
  • Cautionary Terminology: Events are labeled as "consistent with", "suggests", and "indicators" rather than absolute assertions of breach.
  • Compliance: Traffic capture inspection must only occur on authorized networks in compliance with institutional policies and applicable cybersecurity laws.
